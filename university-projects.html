Aircraft design

Outsized Cargo Aircraft

Multidisciplinary aircraft configuration development around an unusual heavy-lift payload and mission.

My contribution

Propulsion · Prize-winning

Two-Shaft Turbofan Engine

A 13.34 kN, Mach 0.82, 35,000 ft business-aircraft engine developed from thermodynamic cycle to mechanical layout.

My contribution

Optimised the GasTurb cycle, iteratively sized the complete gas path and designed the integrated compressor, turbine, shaft, bearing, casing and mount architecture. Awarded the Stan Stevens Prize for best individual performance.

Lightweight Wing Design & Test

Structural concept, manufacture and testing of a lightweight wing designed around strength and mass constraints.

Project focus

Load-path definition, rib and spar geometry, manufacturability and comparison of predicted behaviour with physical test response.

Mechanical design

UH-60 Main Rotor Assembly

Detailed CAD development of a complex helicopter rotor-head mechanism and its moving interfaces.

Project focus

Flight test

Saab 340B+ Flying Laboratory

Flight-test planning, in-flight data collection and analysis aboard the Cranfield research aircraft.

Project focus

UAS design & build

Terrain-Mapping UAV

Team development and manufacture of a small unmanned aircraft for remote-sensing tasks.

Project focus

CFD

Steady & Turbulent Cube Flow

Mesh development, model comparison and interpretation of separated channel flow around a bluff body.

Methods

Compared steady and turbulent assumptions, convergence behaviour, wake structure and the sensitivity of the result to numerical setup.

Sensor fusion

Vehicle Navigation Data Fusion

Processing and fusion of test-vehicle measurements with an OxTS RT3000 inertial/GPS navigation system.

Methods

Aligned multi-sensor time histories, compared measurement quality and assessed how fused estimates changed the interpretation of vehicle motion.

03
Wind tunnel

Turbulent Boundary Layer

Experimental measurement and analysis of velocity profiles and boundary-layer behaviour.

Methods

Reduced wind-tunnel data into engineering quantities, assessed uncertainty and compared measured profiles with expected turbulent behaviour.

04
Flight dynamics

Aircraft Stability & Flight Test

Stability calculations and interpretation of aircraft response from controlled flight-test manoeuvres.

Methods

Linked theoretical derivatives and modes to measured time histories, then assessed the limitations of the test method and data.

05
Turbomachinery

Axial Compressor Surge & Stall

Experimental compressor characterisation focused on operating limits, pressure rise and unstable behaviour.

Methods

Reduced rig measurements, constructed characteristic trends and investigated the onset and signatures of surge and stall.

06
Aerodynamics

NASA Supercritical Aerofoil

Numerical investigation of a supercritical aerofoil across operating conditions relevant to transonic aircraft.

Methods

Compared lift, drag and pressure behaviour while considering the relationship between geometry, shock formation and operating point.

07
Controls

Control Engineering Analysis

Modelling, testing and interpretation of closed-loop system behaviour.

Methods

Compared predicted and measured response, explored controller behaviour and communicated the trade between speed, stability and robustness.

08
Materials

Mechanics of Materials & EBT

Laboratory testing and Euler–Bernoulli beam-theory analysis of structural response.

Methods

Connected measured displacement and strain behaviour with analytical assumptions, measurement uncertainty and model limitations.

09
Dynamics

Ping-Pong Ball Simulation

Dynamics and vibration model of a lightweight ball responding to repeated impacts and motion.

Methods

Developed the governing model, evaluated transient behaviour and tested how parameter assumptions affected the simulated motion.
