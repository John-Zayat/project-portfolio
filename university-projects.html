Aircraft Design

Outsized Cargo Aircraft

Preliminary design of an outsized aircraft for transporting Vestas V150 wind-turbine blades.

My contribution

Calculated CG and landing-gear positions and produced the complete aircraft and cargo CAD assembly, renders and technical views.

Gas Turbine Design · Prize-winning

Two-Shaft Turbofan Engine

Preliminary design of a 13.34 kN two-spool turbofan for Mach 0.82 cruise at 35,000 ft.

My contribution

Optimised the thermodynamic cycle in GasTurb 12, iteratively sized the gas-path annuli and developed the shaft, bearing and casing architecture within geometric, stress and manufacturing constraints; awarded the Stan Stevens Prize for the best individual performance in the Gas Turbine Design module cohort.

Structures

Lightweight Wing Design & Test

Designed, manufactured and tested a lightweight wing structure, comparing predicted and measured deflection.

My contribution

Selected the aerofoil and structure, produced the CAD and manufacturing files, and performed FEA and LR50K testing.

Mechanical Design

UH-60 Main Rotor Assembly

Structurally assessed the UH-60 main-rotor assembly, comparing hand calculations and Simcenter NASTRAN FEA results and ANSYS Granta material selection.

My contribution

Created the CAD assembly and performed blade- and assembly-level tension, torsion, buckling and vibrational analyses.

Flight Test

Saab 340B+ National Flying Laboratory

Analysed full-scale flight-test data from the Saab 340B+ National Flying Laboratory to evaluate aircraft performance.

My contribution

Analysed its drag, climb and cruise performance characteristics and compared the results with the Jetstream 31.

UAS Design & Build

Terrain-Mapping UAV

Designed, manufactured and flight-tested a camera-equipped UAV intended to map terrain while flying a constrained circular path on a 9 m tether.

My contribution

Selected the aerofoil and wing design in XFLR5, produced the CAD and laser-cut files, assembled the airframe and performed a successful maiden flight.

CFD

Steady & Turbulent Cube Flow

Mesh development and comparison of RANS and LES predictions of cube wake behaviour and drag with wind-tunnel data.

Methods

Compared steady and turbulent assumptions, convergence behaviour, wake structure and the sensitivity of the result to numerical setup.

Sensor fusion

Vehicle Navigation Data Fusion

Processing and fusion of test-vehicle measurements with an OxTS RT3000 inertial/GPS navigation system using Kalman Filters.

Methods

Used MATLAB for moving-average and noise analysis, covariance and correlation matrices, weighted pseudo-inverse estimation, Gaussianity testing and the development of a Kalman filter for vehicle-position estimation.

Wind tunnel

Turbulent Boundary Layer

Characterised a turbulent boundary layer from multi-position velocity-probe measurements, determining its thickness, shape factor and skin-friction behaviour.

Methods

Calculated δ, δ* and H using Power-law and numerical-integration methods, estimated skin friction using the Ludwieg–Tillmann correlation, and produced Clauser diagrams for comparison with the MIE method.

Flight dynamics

Aircraft Stability & Flight Test

Stability calculations and interpretation of aircraft response from controlled flight-test manoeuvres.

Methods

Linked theoretical derivatives and modes to measured time histories, then assessed the limitations of the test method and data.

Turbomachinery

Axial Compressor Surge & Stall

Experimental compressor characterisation focused on operating limits, pressure rise and unstable behaviour.

Methods

LabVIEW data acquisition, performance maps, velocity triangles and experimental surge investigation.

Aerodynamics

NASA Supercritical Aerofoil

Assessed a NASA supercritical aerofoil across incidence and Mach-number sweeps using SU2.

Methods

CFD comparison with NASA data, Tecplot visualisation of shock generation and critical and drag-divergence Mach calculations.

Controls

Control Engineering Analysis

Analysed and tuned a fighter-aircraft carrier-landing control model.

Methods

MATLAB and Simulink gain tuning, hand-drawn root-locus plots, stability step-response graphs and Bode Plot analysis.

Materials

Mechanics of Materials & EBT

Compared Elementary Beam Theory with measured cantilever-beam strain and deflection.

Methods

EBT calculations and Excel comparison of theoretical and experimental stress and displacement.

Dynamics

Ping-Pong Ball Simulation

Modelled a ping-pong ball rolling down an incline in Simulink.

Methods

Hand-derived translational and rotational acceleration, drag and terminal-velocity equations implemented in Simulink.

University Team Experience

Loughborough UAV Team

As an aerodynamics engineer, I batch-analysed aerofoils and dihedral/anhedral configurations in XFLR5, completed aircraft stability studies, performed full-airframe STAR-CCM+ simulations and developed an internal EDF S-duct for a 10 kg autonomous tandem-wing UAV.
